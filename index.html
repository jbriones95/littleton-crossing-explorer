How estimates work

We estimate each movement profile's crossing time by dividing the crossing distance by its walking speed and rounding up. The map overlay compares those estimates with reported signal timing when available.

Read the CDOT Guidelines for Pedestrian Signal Timing

Data source: supplied Littleton street-centerline dataset. Estimates are informational only.
